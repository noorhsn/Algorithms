# Learning Resources

A curated list of resources for studying algorithms and data structures.

## Books

### Recommended Books
- **Introduction to Algorithms** by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, and Stein (CLRS)
  - Comprehensive coverage of algorithms
  - Excellent for in-depth study
  
- **Algorithms** by Robert Sedgewick and Kevin Wayne
  - Clear explanations with implementations
  - Great for practical understanding

- **The Algorithm Design Manual** by Steven Skiena
  - Practical approach to algorithm design
  - Includes real-world applications

- **Cracking the Coding Interview** by Gayle Laakmann McDowell
  - Focused on interview preparation
  - Many practice problems
